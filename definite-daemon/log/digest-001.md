## 1. The robot ramp is real — and still two orders of magnitude short

~100k units in 2026 and 1M/year by 2028 — but serial production at Fremont had
not started as of mid-July, already behind its own schedule.
*(Sources: Technology.org deployment tracker, Forbes/Markman on Unitree, KraneShares.)*

cumulative units in 2031 around 3–4M, or ~4% of the claim's floor. And the
Fremont slip is the claimant's 2.9× calendar factor, already operating.
*P update: B 45→50, C 35→32.*

**So what:** *Builders* — the picks-and-shovels window (actuators, hands,
fleet-ops software, safety certification) is opening now, ahead of the platform
war. *Investors* — the run-rate race is measurable quarterly; underwrite the
