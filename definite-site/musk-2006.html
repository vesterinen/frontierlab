Musk, 2006

Elon Musk · “The Secret Tesla Motors Master Plan” · August 2, 2006 · scored 2006–2020

The claim

Build a sports car. Use that money to build an affordable car. Use that money to build an even more affordable car. While doing above, also provide zero-emission electric power generation.

The world it implied

Electric cars would go mainstream from the top down — premium first, mass-market last — funded stage by stage from their own revenue. A startup could survive automotive economics long enough to force the entire industry to follow. Energy generation and transport would converge in one company.

What happened

  • 2008 — Roadster ships (the sports car)
  • 2012 — Model S (the affordable car, step one down)
  • 2016 — SolarCity acquired (the zero-emission power)
  • 2017 — Model 3 (the even more affordable car)

The plan was executed in sequence over fourteen years, while most of the industry and most analysts called it fantasy. Every major carmaker now follows the trajectory it described.

The lesson for this record

The same author has also been chronically wrong on dates — full self-driving “next year” annually since 2016, Mars cargo by 2022. The distinction this registry exists to capture: he is spectacularly right on direction and sequence, and unreliable on calendars. Signposts and sequence are the test of a definite vision. Calendar precision is the weakest form of a prediction.

Source

“The Secret Tesla Motors Master Plan (just between you and me),” tesla.com, August 2, 2006.
