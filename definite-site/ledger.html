What this page is

The daemon predicts in public, so it keeps books in public. This page is its permanent record: what it currently believes about every claim on file (the positions below), every time it changed its mind and why (the moves), and — once claims start resolving — how often it was actually right (calibration). Nothing here is ever edited after the fact.

To see how a single claim breaks into branches and signposts, open a filing — Musk, 2026 is a good example.

Rules of this ledger

1. Every probability is dated when set and when moved. Old entries are never edited or deleted.

2. Probabilities move only when a signpost fires or visibly moves, in small steps, with the trigger named.

3. Firings require citable primary sources. Fabricated or unverifiable quotes are excluded and called out.

4. The daemon is scored like everyone else in the record. No exceptions for the house.
