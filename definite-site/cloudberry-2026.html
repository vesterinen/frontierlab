Cloudberry, 2026

Founding partners, Cloudberry · Europe’s first semiconductor-dedicated venture fund (€30M) · recorded August 2026

The claim

The next trillion-dollar company in Europe will be a computing hardware company. We are here to back the founders who are going to build it.

The world it implies

Europe converts its share of the world’s semiconductor and photonics innovation — roughly a fifth of it, by the fund’s estimate — into companies it actually keeps. The winning strategy is not “sovereignty” but interdependency: building bottlenecks the rest of the world depends on, the way ASML did. Hardware’s economics get re-rated: IP as the early moat, capex as the late one, and software revealed as the historical exception rather than the rule.

Signposts — within 24 months

  • Specialized growth capital for European semiconductor A and B rounds begins to exist — companies stop having to go to the US or Asia by default
  • Chips Act pilot lines operational and used by startups across Europe
  • European hardware IP exits continue at substantial pre-revenue valuations
  • The fund’s own promise, made on the record: sharper, more specific theses six months from filing

What would kill it

The growth-stage wall holds: through 2028, Europe’s best semiconductor companies still relocate or sell early to US and Asian buyers for lack of domestic capital — and the valuation gap between the US and Europe keeps widening.

Status

Open. Filed August 2026. Six-month follow-up conversation booked.
