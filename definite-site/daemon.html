The Definite Daemon

A daemon watches the future

Every future that matters to you is a claim about what happens next. The daemon breaks claims into branches, probabilities, signposts, and kill conditions — then checks them against reality, every day.

The product · Private beta

Paste a claim. Get the tree.

Type any claim about the future — or your company’s mission, or a company URL. The daemon computes the decision tree: the ways it can play out, weighted; the real-world signposts that decide between them; and the condition that kills it.

One tree takes about 30 seconds. Watching it forever is the subscription.

How it works

01

Claim in

A frontier claim, your mission, a company URL — anything definite enough to be checked.

02

Tree out

Branches with probabilities, reasoned from bottlenecks and base rates — not vibes. Signposts dated. Kill condition named.

03

The daemon watches

Subscribed trees are checked against the news daily. When reality moves a branch, you get the ping — with what it means for your seat.

04 · The loop

Reality grades the daemon, too. Every probability is dated and kept; resolved claims become base rates that sharpen the next tree; the daemon’s own record is public on the ledger. We don’t ask you to trust the machine — we show you its books.
