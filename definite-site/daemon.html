How it works

01

Claims get filed

The frontier’s biggest claims about the future — from Musk and Jensen to the founders and investors on our podcast — dated, sourced, on the record.

02

Futures get branched

Each claim becomes a map: the ways it can unfold, the probabilities, and the signposts — real-world events that reveal which branch we’re on.

03

The daemon watches

A machine checks every signpost against the news, daily. When the future moves, you know — with what it means for your seat.

04 · The loop

Reality grades the daemon, too

Every branch probability we publish is dated and kept. When claims resolve, the outcome feeds back: resolved claims become the base rates that sharpen the next tree, and the daemon’s own calls build a track record — scored in public, like everyone else’s. We don’t ask you to trust the machine. We show you its ledger.
