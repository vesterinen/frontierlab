Ville Vesterinen, 2024

Co-founder of Slush / “How to build a trillion-dollar company”
Dated 22.03.2024 / First entry of this record

Trillion-dollar companies are founder-led technology companies — built by people with a definite vision of the future, pursued against a wall of skeptics. Europe will not produce one until it learns to attract and amplify founders.

World implied

Technology keeps compounding as a share of the world economy (5% to 15% of global GDP in the fifteen years before filing). The trillion-dollar club keeps growing — and keeps being dominated by companies still run by the founder who imagined them. Companies that stop seeing a future to build start buying back shares instead. Europe stays absent from the top of the list for as long as its structures repel the people who build such companies.

Signposts
  • New entrants to the trillion-dollar club remain predominantly founder-led technology companies
  • Technology’s share of global GDP keeps rising
  • The ascent gets faster: younger companies reaching the top than the generation before
  • Europe: no entrant without structural change in how it treats founders
Kill condition

A decade in which the new giants are professionally-managed companies without founder vision at the helm — or a Europe that produces a trillion-dollar company without changing anything about how it attracts and amplifies founders.
